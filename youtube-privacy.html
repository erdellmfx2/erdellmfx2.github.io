Hermes_Business YouTube Connector

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026
Operator: Dr. Erdell Maurice

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hermes_Business YouTube Connector for The Formula of Becoming (“Connector,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) accesses, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when the authorized channel owner connects a Google account. The Connector is an owner-operated desktop tool and uses YouTube API Services.

Google user-data disclosure: The Connector accesses YouTube channel and owned-video information only after the account owner gives OAuth consent. It uses that information to display the connected channel, review owned videos and scheduling metadata, set owner-approved thumbnails, and support explicitly approved video uploads. Google user data is not used for advertising or unrelated purposes.

1. Google and YouTube data accessed

Depending on the permissions approved by the channel owner, the Connector may process:

  • the connected YouTube channel ID, title, handle, public description, public subscriber count, video count, and view count;
  • owned-video IDs, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, visibility, processing status, scheduled publication dates, duration, and available view, like, and comment totals;
  • OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, scope information, and token-expiration information needed to maintain the connection;
  • videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags, visibility, and scheduling instructions that the owner explicitly directs the Connector to submit; and
  • API results, timestamps, video or post IDs, URLs, and error messages needed to confirm or troubleshoot an approved action.

The Connector does not request the user's Google password and does not access Gmail, contacts, Google Drive, location, general Google activity, or YouTube watch history.

2. Permissions and purposes

  • youtube.readonly: identifies the authorized channel and reads its channel and owned-video metadata for inventory, scheduling review, verification, and performance summaries.
  • youtube.upload: sets custom thumbnails and may upload owner-approved original videos when that functionality is explicitly enabled and invoked.

The Connector limits its use of Google user data to these disclosed owner-facing functions and security, troubleshooting, and compliance activities necessary to provide them.

3. How data is shared

Data and owner-approved content are transmitted to Google and YouTube only as necessary to complete an authorized API request. Published content is made available according to the YouTube visibility setting selected by the owner. We do not sell Google user data, share it with data brokers, use it for targeted advertising, or allow unrelated third parties to use it.

4. Storage and security

OAuth credentials, tokens, and operational records are kept in access-restricted storage controlled by the operator. Private credential and token files are excluded from public source control and protected by operating-system file permissions. They may be included in the operator's private device backup or synchronization service. No security method is infallible, but reasonable technical and administrative safeguards are used to limit unauthorized access.

5. Retention and deletion

OAuth tokens are retained while the channel connection remains authorized and needed. Operational API data is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the disclosed functions, maintain security, or resolve errors. If the owner revokes consent or requests deletion, authorization tokens and Google-authorized API data associated with that consent will be deleted as soon as practicable and no later than seven calendar days, except information that was independently supplied by the owner and is not Google API Data or information that must be retained by law.

6. Revoking access and requesting deletion

The channel owner can revoke the Connector's Google access through Google Account third-party access settings. The owner may also request revocation assistance or deletion by emailing erdellmfx2@gmail.com. Revocation prevents future authenticated API actions unless the owner authorizes the Connector again.

7. Limited Use

The Connector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

8. YouTube and Google services

The Connector uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own collection and use of information are described in the Google Privacy Policy. This Policy governs the Connector, not information collected independently by Google or YouTube.

9. Children's privacy

The Connector is an account-management tool for an authorized adult channel owner and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the Connector.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Connector's permissions, functions, or data practices change. Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date, and additional consent will be requested when required before Google user data is used for a new purpose.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, revocation assistance, or deletion requests, contact Dr. Erdell Maurice at erdellmfx2@gmail.com.
