Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hermes_Business, a content-publishing integration operated by Dr. Erdell Maurice (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information when an authorized account owner connects a supported social-media account.

1. Information we process

Depending on the permissions you approve and the connected platform, we may process:

  • your public account identifier, display name, and profile information made available by the platform;
  • authorization tokens and related technical data needed to keep the connection active;
  • videos, thumbnails, captions, hashtags, publication settings, and other content you direct us to upload;
  • publication results, such as post IDs, URLs, timestamps, processing status, and error messages; and
  • support communications you send to us.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate the connected account, carry out the publishing action you direct or approve, maintain operational records, diagnose technical issues, protect the integration, and comply with applicable obligations.

3. How information is shared

We share content and limited account information with the social-media platform only as needed to provide the requested publishing function. A platform may make published content public according to the visibility setting you choose. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising unrelated to the integration.

4. Storage and retention

Authorization tokens and operational records are stored on restricted-access systems used to operate the integration. We retain them only while a connection remains active or as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, recordkeeping, or legal obligations. You may revoke platform access at any time; after revocation, we will no longer use the revoked token and will delete or de-identify associated connection data when it is no longer needed.

5. Your choices

You can choose not to connect an account, decline optional permissions, revoke authorization through the applicable platform, request deletion of connection-related data, or contact us with privacy questions. Revoking access may prevent the integration from publishing to that account.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to limit access to connection data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s privacy

Hermes_Business is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this integration. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the integration, contact us so we can address the request.

8. Third-party services

Connected platforms, including TikTok, have their own privacy practices. This Policy does not govern information collected directly by those services; please review their privacy notices.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the integration changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact erdellmfx2@gmail.com.
