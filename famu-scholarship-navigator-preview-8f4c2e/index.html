The four-step path

From preparation to official submission

Students always remain responsible for reviewing their work, following sponsor requirements, and meeting every deadline.

1

File the FAFSA

Complete the 2026–2027 FAFSA and include FAMU school code 001480 when required.

2

Enter AcademicWorks

Use the official FAMU scholarship portal and sign in with the student’s university credentials.

3

Prepare four essays

Develop the personal, financial need, leadership and service, and mathematics research responses.

4

Match and apply

Review the complete packet, follow sponsor instructions, and submit through the official portal.

Inside the student toolkit

One guided preparation workflow

Each tool helps the student produce clearer, more complete materials without replacing student judgment or authorship.

Essay Studio

Guided prompts for the four core essays, grounded in the student’s own experiences and evidence.

Faculty Recommendation Builder

A professional request that gives faculty the course connection, accomplishments, and deadline.

Rubric and Review

A structured readiness check for clarity, evidence, alignment, mechanics, and other evaluation areas.

Application Export

A complete packet students can review before transferring materials to FAMU AcademicWorks.

Student-control safeguard

The Scholarship Navigator prepares and organizes. The student completes every official submission, and sponsor instructions and deadlines always control.
