Hermes_Business

YouTube Publishing Connector

Application: The Formula of Becoming Publisher
Operator: Dr. Erdell Maurice
Last updated: August 28, 2026

The Hermes_Business YouTube Connector is an owner-operated desktop integration used by Dr. Erdell Maurice to manage original educational and mathematics-storytelling content for The Formula of Becoming on the authorized Erdell Maurice YouTube channel.

Plain-language disclosure: When the channel owner approves Google authorization, this connector can read and manage the owner's YouTube channel and owned content and can use the approved upload permission to set custom thumbnails or, when separately and explicitly enabled, upload owner-approved videos. It does not publish, modify, or delete anything merely because an account is connected.

What the connector does

  • identifies the YouTube channel selected by the authenticated owner;
  • reads owned-video titles, IDs, visibility, processing status, scheduled publication dates, and available engagement totals;
  • sets an approved custom thumbnail after checking the exact video and saving a recovery copy of the prior thumbnail; and
  • may later upload original, owner-approved videos only through an explicit protected publishing command.

Google permissions requested

  • YouTube read-only: used to identify the authorized channel and inspect its channel and video metadata.
  • YouTube upload: used to set custom video thumbnails and, if the owner later enables the feature, upload owner-approved videos.
  • YouTube account management: permits owner-directed management of owned videos, playlists, subscriptions, channel sections, and live-video features. Destructive connector commands are disabled by default and require temporary local enablement plus exact confirmation.

What the connector does not do

  • It does not request or store the owner's Google password.
  • It does not access the owner's email, contacts, Drive files, location, general Google activity, or YouTube watch history.
  • It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, credit, surveillance, or unrelated profiling.
  • It does not upload or publish content without a direct owner instruction or an owner-approved scheduled workflow.
  • It does not delete a video or playlist unless the owner temporarily enables the matching private safety switch and explicitly confirms the exact resource.

Security and user control

OAuth credentials and tokens are kept in access-restricted storage controlled by the operator and excluded from the public website repository. The channel owner can revoke access at any time through Google Account third-party access settings or request deletion by email.
